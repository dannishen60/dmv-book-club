Finance books Danni recommends

Alongside the club's picks, these three come up often in the group. Each suits a different question.

  • Investing and trading

    Trade Your Way to Financial Freedom

    Van K. Tharp

    The place to start for investment and trading, although it does involve quite a bit of math.

  • Personal finance

    The Psychology of Money

    Morgan Housel

    For personal finance and the habits around money, this is the one to read first.

  • Investment philosophy

    Poor Charlie's Almanack

    Charles T. Munger

    Worth reading for investment philosophy. It covers a number of useful mental models and discusses various behavioral finance biases.
